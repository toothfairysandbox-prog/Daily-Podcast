The Buffalo Bills beat the Kansas City Chiefs thirteen to ten on Thanksgiving night, and it was every bit as ugly and tense as that score suggests. Freezing rain turned the field into a mess, both offenses sputtered for long stretches, and Kansas City had chances late to tie or win it but couldn't finish. For Buffalo, it's a statement in a game that's become an annual measuring stick against Patrick Mahomes. For the Chiefs, it's another reminder that this season has not gone the way it was supposed to.

The other marquee Thanksgiving game was a disaster for Dallas. The Cowboys collapsed against the Eagles in a way that's now becoming a pattern — their second franchise-level meltdown this season — and with it, realistically, goes any hope of an NFC East title. The vultures are circling in Dallas this morning.

Off the field, the Aaron Rodgers saga keeps generating headlines days after Pittsburgh waived him. Tom Brady went after Rodgers for letting a private dispute with Mike McCarthy become public, while Russell Wilson sided with his old head coach on the whole mess. It's dominating NFL conversation more than almost any actual game right now.

In the NBA, LeBron James posted a triple-double — twenty-five points, twelve assists, ten rebounds — to push the Sixers past the Heat in NBA Cup group play, though Philadelphia will be without Jaylen Brown again with his hamstring injury. Friday brings an action-packed final day of NBA Cup group play with eleven games deciding who advances.

Looking ahead, Sunday's Jaguars-Titans game and the rest of Week Twelve carry real playoff-picture weight, especially after Kansas City's stumble. Tonight, keep an eye on the NBA Cup bracket shaking out — several spots are still up for grabs.
