Stocks are rallying to end a rough week, fueled by what traders call a Nvidia lifeline after the stock's twenty-seven percent monthly slide — though Nvidia just halted server shipments to four Southeast Asian countries, and Jensen Huang faces Congress this week. Fifteen minutes in, the S&P 500 is up seven tenths of a percent, the Nasdaq nearly one percent, and the Russell 2000 leading, up over one percent. Asian markets are surging even harder — Shanghai up nearly four percent, South Korea's Kospi over four percent. Energy and communication services lead sectors, both up about one and four tenths percent, classic risk-on buying. The ten-year yield fell six basis points to four eighty-five, a bull steepener as growth fears ease.
