Researchers have engineered a kind of molecular glue that restores normal chromosome connections in aging mouse eggs. As eggs age, the structures holding chromosome pairs together weaken, which is a big reason fertility drops and miscarriage risk rises with age. In this study, the engineered protein system stabilized those connections in mice. That's encouraging for eventually improving IVF outcomes, but it's a proof-of-concept in mice, not a eggs-in-humans result yet, so treat this as early and promising rather than imminent.

A few other things worth knowing. Scientists have coaxed a tobacco-relative plant to actually synthesize paclitaxel, the chemotherapy drug normally extracted at great cost and environmental toll from yew trees. It's a proof-of-concept study, but if it scales, it could make a major cancer drug cheaper and more sustainable to produce. Separately, a study recording from forty-three regions of the mouse brain found that most neurons aren't narrow specialists — they respond to a wide range of information in flexible ways. That challenges a long-standing assumption about how brains are organized. And new research points to two specific windows, infancy and the start of formal schooling, when heavier screen time was linked to weaker academic performance and working memory years later. It's an association from observational data, not proof that screens caused the deficits, but the timing specificity is notable.

On the multitasking front: a study found men and women are similarly capable overall, but men were more than twice as likely to tune out conversation while juggling tasks — which may be the real driver behind the old stereotype that women multitask better. Worth a little skepticism before you crown anyone the better multitasker based on one study design.

What to watch: the tick-borne alpha-gal allergy to red meat keeps drawing more diagnoses with still-murky biology, and researchers are pushing to understand why. And if you want something practical this weekend, there's a short quiz out now assessing dementia risk factors — not a diagnosis, just a nudge toward habits linked to brain health.
