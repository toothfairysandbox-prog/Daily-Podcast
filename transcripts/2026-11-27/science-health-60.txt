Researchers engineered a molecular glue that restores normal chromosome connections in aging mouse eggs — a possible path toward better IVF outcomes down the line, though this is proof-of-concept in mice, not a human treatment. Separately, scientists coaxed a tobacco-relative plant to synthesize paclitaxel, a major chemotherapy drug normally extracted expensively from yew trees — early but promising for cutting cost and environmental toll.

Also notable: a study recording activity across forty-three mouse brain regions found most neurons are flexible generalists rather than narrow specialists, challenging assumptions about how brains are wired.
