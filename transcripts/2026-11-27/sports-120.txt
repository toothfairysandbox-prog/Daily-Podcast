The Buffalo Bills beat Kansas City thirteen to ten on Thanksgiving night in miserable, freezing rain, with the Chiefs unable to convert chances late to save it. It's another measuring-stick win for Buffalo and another sign Kansas City's season isn't going to plan.

The other marquee Thanksgiving game was a disaster for Dallas, who collapsed against the Eagles in their second franchise-level meltdown this season. Their NFC East hopes are effectively done.

Off the field, the Aaron Rodgers situation keeps rolling days after Pittsburgh waived him — Tom Brady criticized him for letting a private dispute with Mike McCarthy go public, while Russell Wilson backed the coach's side.

In the NBA, LeBron James put up a triple-double, twenty-five points, twelve assists, ten rebounds, to lead the Sixers past the Heat in NBA Cup play, though Jaylen Brown remains out with a hamstring issue. Friday is the final day of NBA Cup group play, with eleven games deciding who moves on.

Sunday's Jaguars-Titans matchup carries real weight in a tightening playoff picture. Tonight, watch how that NBA Cup bracket shakes out.
