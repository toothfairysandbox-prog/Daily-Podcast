Jensen Huang disappeared from view for eleven days this month, and Nvidia's board failed the resulting succession test — there's no clear number two, so the AI trade still rides on one man. He's back, and this morning AI stocks rebounded hard, with futures surging after weeks that left the Nasdaq 100 headed for its worst month in nearly four years.
