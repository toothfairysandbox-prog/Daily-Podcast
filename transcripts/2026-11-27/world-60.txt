Ukraine's Volodymyr Zelensky told the BBC the Trump-Putin relationship is "strange," saying Washington isn't pressing Russia hard enough to end the war. That came as EU leaders left a Brussels summit without agreeing on a budget or a forty-nine billion dollar loan for Ukraine, while Russia keeps battering Ukraine's power grid ahead of another hard winter.

Separately, North Korea's Kim Jong Un responded to a recent Trump overture by visiting nuclear material facilities and vowing to build more weapons, citing threats from the US and South Korea.
