Russia launched one of its biggest air attacks yet on Ukraine, killing at least six in Kyiv, and two missiles landed in Poland — called a provocation by Warsaw, though NATO territory saw no damage. It followed Putin's vow of a "painful" response to Ukraine's Crimea bridge strike, and comes as Putin separately proposes evacuating Kostiantynivka, a plan Kyiv suspects masks a military operation.

Separately, Britain and the EU struck a landmark deal easing trade checks, linking carbon markets, and opening youth travel between them, a decade after Brexit.
