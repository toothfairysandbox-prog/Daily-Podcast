A U.C. Berkeley compound, tested in mice, burns fat by boosting energy expenditure up to eighteen percent rather than suppressing appetite like Ozempic or Wegovy, while sparing muscle. It's promising, but it's mice, not people, so don't read too much into it yet. Separately, a Nature study found resistance exercise helps older adults preserve muscle while on weight-loss drugs, something people can actually act on today, unlike the mouse compound.
