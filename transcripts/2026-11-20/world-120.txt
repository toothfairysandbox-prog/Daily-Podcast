Russia launched one of its biggest air attacks yet on Ukraine overnight, killing at least six in Kyiv, and two Russian missiles landed in southeastern Poland — which Warsaw's prime minister called a provocation against NATO territory, though no damage was reported. It came hours after Putin vowed a "painful" response to Ukraine's strike on the Crimea bridge. European leaders meeting in Berlin say they have a united position for Russia talks, but Putin's separate proposal to evacuate the city of Kostiantynivka is being read by Kyiv and analysts as military cover, not humanitarian relief.

Britain and the EU struck a landmark post-Brexit deal, scrapping checks on plant and animal trade, linking carbon markets, and opening a youth mobility scheme for a hundred thousand people. Prime Minister Andy Burnham calls it a step toward undoing a decade of damage.

The International Court of Justice ruled Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, ordering reparations. Myanmar and China reject the ruling; Rohingya groups say real change is still far off.

At COP31, talks ran into overtime with the draft deal still omitting any mention of fossil fuels.
