President Trump's administration has proposed new tariffs on the European Union, Japan, Taiwan and China, with Beijing facing a possible twenty percent rate. It comes just weeks after a cost-of-living backlash handed Republicans a sweeping midterm defeat, and signals no retreat from trade confrontation. Expect pushback from Brussels and Beijing.

Leaked Russian data shows Moscow has lost nearly five hundred thousand people in the Ukraine war, revealing how deeply the invasion has reshaped the country's mortality patterns. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy is pushing for fast-tracked NATO membership and more air defense, but a NATO Parliamentary Assembly meeting in Berlin showed the required unanimity is still missing. North Korea, meanwhile, appears to be holding back on sending Russia more troops, with analysts saying Pyongyang is balancing ties with both Moscow and Beijing.

In Colombia, security forces freed around twenty hostages held for seventeen hours at a Cartagena supermarket; one gunman died in the raid. The captors were common criminals fleeing a botched armored truck heist, the president said, not part of a wider threat.

Watch China's politics too: Beijing is investigating Politburo member and Shanghai party chief Chen Jining, the latest big name caught in Xi Jinping's anti-corruption drive.
