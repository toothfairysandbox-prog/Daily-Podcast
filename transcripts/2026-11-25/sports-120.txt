Aaron Rodgers didn't show for the Steelers' Wednesday meeting, a day after Mike McCarthy benched him, and he skipped the team facility too. The benching blindsided the locker room, and the growing read around the league is that this could be the last game the four-time MVP ever plays. McCarthy is expected to address it publicly at some point today.

The bigger football story this week is Thanksgiving. Bills and Chiefs renew their rivalry Thursday with Buffalo at nine and one atop the AFC and Kansas City a game back — it's being called the biggest regular-season game of the year. Packers and Rams play tonight with real NFC wild-card stakes, the Rams dealing with injuries at receiver. Bears-Lions and Boston College-Miami round out the holiday slate.

Off the field, Raheem Sterling, the former England international, got a suspended twelve-month jail sentence for dangerous driving and nitrous oxide possession after a motorway crash.

The Rodgers story is the one with legs here — four MVPs to an unexplained benching almost overnight, and now a real question of whether anyone claims him or this is just how it ends.

Tonight, Packers-Rams carries the most on-field stakes; tomorrow, Bills-Chiefs is the one worth clearing your schedule for.
