Inflation came in hot — the Fed's preferred gauge hit 3.8% annually in October — and it's combining with fallout from Nvidia's Jensen Huang saga, where his resignation and reinstatement under reported White House pressure is drawing scrutiny over tech's political ties, to send stocks sharply lower. Sixteen minutes into the session, the S&P 500 is down three percent, the Nasdaq down four and a half, the Dow off two percent. South Korea's KOSPI is down over six percent overnight. Technology is the worst sector, down over five percent, with Dell falling despite doubling sales. The ten-year yield actually eased slightly to 4.91%, so this looks like an inflation scare, not a growth scare.
