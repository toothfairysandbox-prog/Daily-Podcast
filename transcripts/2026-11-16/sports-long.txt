The biggest scoreline of the night came out of Berlin, where Germany put five past the Netherlands without reply in Nations League play. Jürgen Klopp called it an extraordinary performance, and it was built on a tactical switch he made to deal with how the Dutch were setting up. Florian Wirtz was at the heart of it and says the team needs to build on the momentum rather than treat it as a one-off. It's the kind of result that changes how a group is talked about heading into the new year.

Elsewhere in the Nations League, Scotland's night went the opposite direction. A four-nil defeat to Switzerland dropped them to third in their group, and captain Andy Robertson, who broke the country's caps record in the match, didn't hold back afterward, saying Scotland simply don't have time to be patient with the rebuild. In the NFL, the Lions lost edge rusher Aidan Hutchinson for the rest of the season and responded by claiming Tyrus Wheat off waivers to shore up the pass rush. In Indianapolis, coach Shane Steichen is holding firm on Daniel Jones at quarterback despite the struggles, with a short week ahead and a Thursday night trip to Houston. And in the NBA, Cleveland will be without Evan Mobley for at least another week with a toe injury, while San Antonio keeps rolling, their young core extending the win streak to twelve games behind another strong week from Wembanyama and company.

The story worth watching is that Spurs streak. Twelve straight with Victor Wembanyama, Stephon Castle and Harper all contributing is not a fluke pattern this deep into a season, and it's starting to look like the clearest sign yet of how good this core can be once it matures. Keep an eye on whether anyone in the West can actually slow them down.

On the schedule tonight, Monday Night Football has the Chargers hosting the Ravens, a game with real playoff seeding weight on both sides. In the NBA, Boston hosts Orlando. And in soccer news, Declan Rice has verbally agreed a new long-term contract at Arsenal, which is the kind of off-field story that matters more than most Monday results.
