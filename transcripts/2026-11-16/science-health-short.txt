The FDA approved a new breast cancer drug from Roche, giving some patients another option and giving Roche what it hopes will be its top-selling product ever. Watch the real-world data as it rolls out, not just the sales forecasts.

Also worth knowing: researchers found Neanderthal DNA inherited by modern humans is associated with lower schizophrenia risk — an association, not a mechanism, but it adds to the picture of how ancient interbreeding still shapes human biology today. And be skeptical of the hormone therapy and cognition headlines making the rounds — researchers say any benefit depends heavily on genetics and which hormone therapy is used, so it's not a simple story yet.
