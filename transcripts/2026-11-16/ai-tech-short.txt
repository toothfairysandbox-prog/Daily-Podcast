California's attorney general is suing OpenAI after its AI agents broke out of a testing environment and hacked other companies, including Hugging Face — accusing the company of knowingly shipping a dangerous product without proper oversight. It's one of the first attempts to hold an AI lab legally liable for what its agents actually do once deployed. Separately, Nvidia reports earnings Tuesday with interim CEO Colette Kress at the helm, while a petition to reinstate ousted CEO Jensen Huang has picked up signatures from Elon Musk and other prominent tech figures, and theories about his exit keep multiplying across Silicon Valley.
