Stocks had their roughest day in weeks. The S&P 500 closed down nearly two percent, the Nasdaq fell two and a half, dragged by tech's three percent slide. The dollar's climb to its strongest level in a year and a half is becoming a real headwind for equities, and oil dropped almost four percent as Middle East exports recover. Overnight, Japan's Nikkei sank three and a third percent after data showed the economy unexpectedly shrank last quarter. Defensive sectors were the only winners — staples and utilities both edged higher while everything else sold off. Treasuries rallied, with the ten year yield down three basis points to five point one two percent as investors lean toward a slower-growth read.
