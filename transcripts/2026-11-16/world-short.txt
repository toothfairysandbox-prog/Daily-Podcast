Donald Trump is in Asia for a three-way sit-down with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin at the ASEAN summit in Manila, his first foreign trip since Republicans' midterm drubbing — a sign he may be leaning into foreign policy now that his domestic power is weaker. Meanwhile Ukraine launched its largest drone barrage of the war, over two thousand drones in a day by Russia's count, hitting the Crimea bridge and triggering a record seventeen air raid alerts in Russia. Ukrainian officials say the message is simple: hit our bridges, we'll hit yours. Separately, five EU countries — Germany, Austria, the Netherlands, Denmark and Greece — unveiled a plan to deport rejected asylum seekers to a new hub in Rwanda.
