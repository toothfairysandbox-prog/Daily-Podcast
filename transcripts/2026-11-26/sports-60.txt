Aaron Rodgers' time in Pittsburgh is over. The Steelers benched him, he blasted head coach Mike McCarthy publicly, and the team released him within hours — he may have played his last NFL game. McCarthy's own job is now in question over the next several games. Elsewhere, the Rams ran all over the Packers behind Blake Corum, and Duke beat UConn seventy-seven to sixty-three, dropping the Huskies to two losses on the season. Tonight is Thanksgiving football: Bears at Lions for first place in the NFC North, then Bills at Chiefs, with Buffalo chasing a fifth straight win over Kansas City.
