Norse Atlantic Airways has suspended all flights. The budget transatlantic carrier, only five years old, warned it faces bankruptcy without an emergency rescue, and rising fuel costs are what tipped it over. It grounded planes on Thanksgiving, stranding passengers mid-holiday, and is now in last-ditch talks for funding. It's a reminder that the budget long-haul airline model has always run on thin margins, and fuel is the thing that breaks it first.

Altria is considering a hundred-fifty-seven-billion-dollar takeover of Imperial Brands, the London-listed maker of Winston and Davidoff. If it happens, it would be one of the biggest tobacco tie-ups ever, consolidating two companies managing the same long decline in cigarette volumes by combining scale instead of fighting each other for it. Separately, Nvidia has reportedly halted AI chip shipments to Southeast Asia as Washington tightens curbs on China-linked transshipment, days after Jensen Huang returned as chief executive following a personal scandal involving a suspected Chinese spy. And UniCredit's takeover of Commerzbank has cleared its last regulatory hurdle, with Poland signing off and CEO Andrea Orcel now seeking the chair role himself — European banking consolidation, after years of talk, is actually happening.

In Britain, millions face a roughly hundred-pound monthly rise in mortgage payments as more borrowers roll off fixed deals than previously estimated, and separately, millions more could be owed compensation over car finance commission arrangements between lenders and dealers. Put those together with American consumers increasingly shopping secondhand for value this holiday season, and Amazon warehouse workers on food stamps tripling despite record company revenue, and you've got the same story everywhere: households absorbing costs that used to sit somewhere else. Watch whether UK wage growth catches up before more fixed deals expire — if it does, this eases; if not, it compounds.

Keep an eye on delivery pricing too — DoorDash, Uber Eats and Walmart are all racing to waive fees and bundle orders as that market gets more competitive heading into the holidays.
