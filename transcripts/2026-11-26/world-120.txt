Poland says it will build a no-fly zone along its Ukraine border after repeated drone incursions it blames on Russia — a buffer zone, not just a protest. It fits a wider pattern: Ukraine has been striking Russian ships in the Baltic and Mediterranean to pressure Moscow and reassure Western allies, while Russia develops its own Starlink rival, called Rasvet, to counter Ukraine's satellite edge. The war keeps widening geographically.

In eastern Congo, fighting between rebels and the army has forced Ebola responders to evacuate just as the outbreak passes twelve thousand cases and over five thousand seven hundred deaths, the deadliest on record there. Containment work is now actively disrupted by the violence.

North Korea's Kim Jong Un has vowed to build more nuclear weapons after ignoring a Trump overture, reportedly touring facilities that produce nuclear material.

Myanmar says it won't comply with parts of the International Court of Justice's genocide ruling over the Rohingya — an open rejection of a binding world court judgment.
