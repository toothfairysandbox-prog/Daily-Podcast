Aaron Rodgers' time in Pittsburgh is over. The Steelers benched the four-time MVP, he fired back at head coach Mike McCarthy publicly, and the team released him within hours. It's a stunning collapse, and it leaves McCarthy's own job on the line — reports say the Steelers are standing by him for now, but the next stretch of games will decide his future. Rodgers may have played his last NFL snap.

Elsewhere, the Rams ran all over the Packers behind Blake Corum in an impressive win. In college basketball, Duke beat UConn seventy-seven to sixty-three, the Huskies' second loss of the season, and their fans are not happy with what they're seeing. In the Premier League, Manchester United keeper Senne Lammens is under fire after a rough Champions League night against Sporting, and Pochettino has made his call on Lammens' future ahead of this weekend's Brentford match.

The story to watch is McCarthy in Pittsburgh — a coach without his starting quarterback and under open scrutiny, with the next seven games deciding whether he's still there beyond this season.
