The Russia-Ukraine war is spreading beyond its borders. Russian cruise missiles landed in Poland last week with no Kremlin response, and NATO is reassuring eastern members as Russian incursions into alliance territory rise. Ukraine says it sank a Russian warship docked in Libya, extending the fight far from the front lines.

In Gaza, an Israeli strike killed Naeem Abu Nahel, the territory's internal security chief, along with six others, the latest targeted killing inside the ceasefire framework. And in Colombia, a car bomb wounded more than twenty people amid a string of attacks on security and government buildings, raising fears for the country's decade-old peace deal.
