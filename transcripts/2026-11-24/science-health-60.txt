Hepatitis B vaccination rates for newborns are dropping sharply in the US — real surveillance data, not a study, and worrying because the virus can pass from mother to baby at birth. It's part of a wider pattern: parents refusing newborn vitamin K shots too, and CDC flu vaccination data now coming out monthly instead of weekly, just as flu season ramps up.
