Jensen Huang is back as Nvidia's CEO, eleven days after being ousted over an alleged affair with a Chinese spy. The market added almost a trillion dollars to Nvidia's value on the news, but Trump's claim he "convinced" the board to reinstate Huang drew backlash, and analysts remain split on whether this was pragmatic or reckless — the board still hasn't named a succession plan. A reported SEC probe into finance chief Colette Kress adds more scrutiny.

Also: CrowdStrike is buying Zscaler for forty-four billion dollars in stock, consolidating cybersecurity around AI threats, and Best Buy posted its best comparable sales growth in five years, raising full-year guidance.
