San Antonio's twelve-game win streak ended last night. The Pistons won a hundred and five to ninety-three despite Victor Wembanyama's twenty-five points, eighteen rebounds, and a self-assisted dunk off the backboard that's already everywhere. Detroit took over when he sat, led by Cunningham and Jenkins. Worse for the Spurs: guard Dylan Harper, having a breakout first season as a starter, slipped on a wet spot late and limped off with an apparent groin injury.

Elsewhere, the Knicks beat the Raptors a hundred and eleven to a hundred and two — Toronto's fifteenth straight loss to New York. Minnesota beat Portland behind a LaMelo Ball steal and a game-saving Trey Lyles block, even with Portland's Ayo Dosunmu scoring a season-high thirty. And the Lakers' Luka Dončić continued his historic start hosting Charlotte, though his status tonight against Utah depends on soreness.

In cricket, England swept to a statement series win over Australia, Ben Duckett's century and Sonny Baker's early wickets driving a ninety-seven run win in the Hobart decider. But the louder story is Cameron Bancroft directly accusing Pat Cummins of knowing about the 2018 sandpaper plot in advance — explosive claims putting real pressure on the Australian captain to respond.
