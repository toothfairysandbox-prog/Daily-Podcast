Nvidia posted faster growth than expected in its first earnings call without Jensen Huang, led by interim chief Colette Kress. Results were strong, but Huang won't return, and the SEC is now reportedly probing the company around his exit — a strong business sitting under an unresolved leadership question.

Elsewhere, Target posted its best sales growth in five years and raised guidance, while Lowe's also raised its outlook on strong online sales despite a weak housing market — real evidence, not just promises, that consumers are still spending.
