The United States has officially lost its measles elimination status, a designation held since 2000. The Pan American Health Organization says a measles strain has now circulated continuously here for over a year, the formal line between "eliminated" and "endemic" — driven by record case counts and falling vaccination rates. It's a real erosion of public health standing, not a sudden new personal risk.

One more: a Finnish study tracking kids for eight years found heavier screen use was linked to better teen cognitive processing, challenging the assumption that more screen time is automatically harmful — though what kids actually do on screens likely matters more than the hours.
