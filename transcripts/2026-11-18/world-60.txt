Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin meet Thursday for their first-ever three-way summit, on the sidelines of APEC in Shenzhen. Xi greeted Trump on arrival; Xi also used his summit speech to warn against protectionism, a clear jab at Washington, while Putin arrives trying to move past a Ukrainian strike that collapsed a prized Russian bridge. No deals expected, but the optics matter.

Also: Kuwait says it repelled an Iranian attack for the first time in two months, and Bahrain reports being hit too, after a US strike on Iran's Qeshm island — the US-Iran standoff keeps flaring.
