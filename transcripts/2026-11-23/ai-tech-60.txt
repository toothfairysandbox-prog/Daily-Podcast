Nvidia's board has reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive, just over a week after he resigned amid allegations of affairs, including with a direct report. The board called the vote unanimous, citing satisfaction with his candor, and reporting says senior US officials backed the reversal. The real story is the instability: the world's most valuable company went without settled leadership during a critical chip-supply race, and the board decided it couldn't risk the alternative.

Separately, Sierra, the AI customer experience company co-founded by Bret Taylor, raised one point seven five billion dollars, doubling its valuation to thirty billion in six months, a clear sign enterprise AI spending is accelerating.
