Cincinnati hosts Washington tonight on Monday Night Football with a chance to stretch their lead atop the AFC North, and the story of this Bengals team right now is Joe Burrow, who's been putting up numbers that are genuinely historic for a quarterback, not just hot. Washington's pass defense has improved, but stopping the league's best passing offense on the road is a different test entirely.

Sunday was messy for a few AFC North contenders. The Steelers lost again in Philadelphia, bumbling their way out of it, and that opened the door for Cleveland, who moved into second place in the division during their bye week without lifting a finger. Elsewhere, the Cardinals were throttled by Kansas City and took a flat "F" grade for the effort. The 49ers lost tackle Colton McKivitz for the season to an ankle injury, pushing Vederian Lowe into the lineup. Giants rookie Abdul Carter made good on his coach's prediction that sacks were coming, breaking out with a big game and plenty to say about it afterward. And a disputed non-catch call involving Parker Washington in Las Vegas drew pointed commentary from Jevón Holland, even as the league confirmed the ruling was correct.

In the NBA, Victor Wembanyama's fifty-point game is still the talk of the league a month into the season, with San Antonio sitting at the top of the power rankings. Boston's slide continued with an ugly loss in Golden State, while Washington snapped a losing run with a twenty-point win over Orlando, their third straight victory.

On the European football side, Champions League matchday five kicks off this week, with Real Madrid undefeated through four games and confirming their squad to face PSV Eindhoven Tuesday at the Bernabeu. Manchester City host Napoli with Jeremy Doku relishing a reunion against old teammate Kevin De Bruyne. And Manchester United are still picking up the pieces after getting thumped four-nil by Liverpool, with Pochettino's start about as rough as it gets and the club now appealing Leny Yoro's red card from that game.

Tonight, it's all about Cincinnati and Washington under the lights, with first place in the division effectively up for grabs.
