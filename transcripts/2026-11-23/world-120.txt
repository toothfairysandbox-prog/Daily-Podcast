Ukraine has confirmed it sank a Russian warship, the corvette Merkury, off Libya on Sunday — seven sea drones launched from a mothership, in an operation President Volodymyr Zelensky claimed personally. The Kremlin is warning of "escalation" but won't directly address the strike. It's a signal that Kyiv can now hit Russian assets far from the front line, part of a wider pattern of this war spilling beyond Ukraine's borders, alongside missile incidents over Poland and alleged Russian activity inside Denmark. At home, a Russian strike on Dymer killed eleven people; rescuers pulled a seven-year-old boy alive from the rubble.

The longest United Nations climate conference on record ended in Antalya with no deal, nations split over fossil fuels. Australia's prime minister, co-host of COP31, blamed "difficult geopolitical times." Critics say the process now shields fossil-fuel interests while vulnerable countries pay the price.

In Belgium, a national strike against austerity has cancelled flights and trains, with the government's survival in doubt. And Iran says it will use "all available means" against what it calls a US naval blockade — language sharpening fast without yet tipping into open confrontation.
