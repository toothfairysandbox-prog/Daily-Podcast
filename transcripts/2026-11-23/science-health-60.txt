A 244 patient Phase 2 trial found Kymera Therapeutics' experimental pill effectively treated eczema, raising the prospect of an oral rival to the injectable drug Dupixent. It's solid mid-stage data, not proof it beats Dupixent outright — bigger trials are still ahead.

Also: a phase three trial found an erectile dysfunction drug showed early promise against Alzheimer's disease. That's one early trial, and Alzheimer's has a long history of promising early data that didn't hold up. Worth watching, not a breakthrough.
