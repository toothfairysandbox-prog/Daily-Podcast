Cincinnati hosts Washington tonight on Monday Night Football, looking to stretch their AFC North lead. Joe Burrow is in the middle of a season some are calling the best a quarterback's ever had, and Washington's improved pass defense faces its toughest test yet.

Sunday was rough for a few AFC North hopefuls. The Steelers lost again in Philadelphia, which let Cleveland slide into second place in the division during their bye. The Cardinals were throttled by Kansas City, earning a flat "F" grade. The 49ers lost tackle Colton McKivitz for the season with an ankle injury. Giants rookie Abdul Carter broke out with a big sack game, making good on his coach's prediction. And a disputed non-catch call in Las Vegas drew sharp words from Jevón Holland, even though the league confirmed the call was right.

In the NBA, Victor Wembanyama's fifty-point game is still the headline a month in, with San Antonio topping the power rankings. Boston's slide continued in Golden State, while Washington beat Orlando by twenty for a third straight win.

In European football, Champions League matchday five is underway, with Real Madrid unbeaten through four games ahead of facing PSV Eindhoven Tuesday, and Manchester United still reeling from a four-nil loss to Liverpool under new boss Pochettino.

Tonight, it's Bengals and Commanders, with first place in the division on the line.
