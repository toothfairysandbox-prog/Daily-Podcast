Ukraine has confirmed sinking a Russian warship off Libya on Sunday, the corvette Merkury, using sea drones launched from a mothership. President Volodymyr Zelensky claimed it directly — a signal Kyiv can now strike Russian assets far beyond the front line. The Kremlin warns of "escalation" but won't confirm details. It's part of a pattern of this war reaching beyond Ukraine, alongside missile incidents near Poland and alleged Russian activity in Denmark. Separately, the longest United Nations climate conference on record ended in Antalya with no deal, countries split over fossil fuels, a failure critics say protects fossil-fuel interests at the expense of vulnerable nations.
