The FDA approved a new Roche breast cancer drug, a real option for some patients, though how well it performs broadly is still to be seen. Separately, scientists linked Neanderthal DNA to lower schizophrenia risk in modern humans — a genetic association, not a cause, but part of a growing picture of ancient DNA's effect on mental health today. And hormone therapy after menopause may help cognition, researchers found, though the benefit depends on which hormone and on genetics — not a reason to start treatment on its own.
