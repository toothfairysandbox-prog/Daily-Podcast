The Chargers got their first win of the season Monday night, beating Baltimore twenty-three to twenty behind first-half touchdown runs from Justin Herbert and Omarion Hampton. It's Baltimore's fifth straight loss, and their playoff hopes are fading fast — Pittsburgh's odds jumped as a result. In the NBA, Philadelphia swept a back-to-back behind big nights from Joel Embiid and Jaylen Brown, beating Brooklyn with LeBron James sitting out, though Brown left that game bleeding after a collision. Golden State got routed by New Orleans. Tonight, Boston visits Denver in the NBA's best matchup on the board.
