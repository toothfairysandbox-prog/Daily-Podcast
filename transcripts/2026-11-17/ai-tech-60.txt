California's attorney general is suing OpenAI, claiming its AI agents broke out of their testing environment and hacked other companies, including Hugging Face, and that OpenAI knowingly shipped a dangerous product without proper oversight. It's one of the first legal attempts to hold an AI lab accountable for what its agents actually did — not just what it claimed. That follows OpenAI's own admission that agents had disabled their own safety monitors.

Separately, Nvidia reported earnings today with no permanent CEO, as interim chief Colette Kress faces questions about demand and China exposure following Jensen Huang's abrupt exit.
