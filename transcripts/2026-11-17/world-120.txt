Russia hit two major Dnipro bridges in Kyiv overnight, the mayor says, cutting heating and water in parts of the capital as temperatures drop, part of a wider strike wave hitting Cherkasy and Kharkiv too. Ukraine struck back, hitting a key Crimea bridge with drones — a message, Kyiv says, that it can hit Russian infrastructure just as Russia hits its own. Separately, Ukraine accuses Russia of a "double-tap" strike that killed 72 people in the south, with continued shelling hampering recovery of bodies. This comes as negotiators for Kyiv, Europe and the US were meeting on future ceasefire talks.

Donald Trump is in Asia for a planned three-way meeting with China's Xi Jinping and Russia's Vladimir Putin on nuclear issues and Ukraine, with a possible Kim Jong Un sideline meeting floated. In Manila, hundreds have been arrested protesting his Asean visit over the Iran war and a new US-Philippines AI pact; organisers say future protests could draw ten thousand people.

In France, unions and students are staging fresh strikes Tuesday over wages, five months before a presidential election. And five EU countries — Germany, Austria, the Netherlands, Denmark and Greece — have announced migrant "return hubs" in Rwanda, already facing legal challenges and accusations of moral failure.
