Nvidia's board says it won't reinstate Jensen Huang as chief executive, despite pressure from billionaire allies and Donald Trump backing the push, and despite its own letter warning of "irreplaceable" talent loss. Inquiries into Huang's relationship with a woman suspected of working with Chinese intelligence are still ongoing, likely why the board is holding firm. Interim chief Colette Kress now runs Nvidia's first earnings call without him, with investors watching closely.

Separately, Chase and Wells Fargo customers were locked out of their accounts Monday by a software bug, the second major tech failure in days after outages grounded flights nationwide and left airlines tallying the cost of more than twenty thousand canceled flights.
