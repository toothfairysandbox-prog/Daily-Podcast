The Los Angeles Chargers were winless through nine weeks. Monday night they beat Baltimore, twenty-three to twenty, with Justin Herbert and Omarion Hampton both running for first-half touchdowns while the Chargers dominated at the line. For the Ravens it's five straight losses now, with a leaky run defense and Jacoby Brissett making a rough quarterback debut. Pittsburgh's playoff odds jumped as a direct result.

In the NBA, Philadelphia is back atop the East after sweeping a back-to-back, with Joel Embiid and Jaylen Brown both scoring heavily in a win at Brooklyn — though Brown left bleeding after colliding with Julius Randle, and LeBron James sat that game out. Cleveland leaned on its bench depth to beat Chicago, and Golden State got blown out by New Orleans, with Steve Kerr already promising lineup changes.

Baltimore's collapse is the thing to watch now — five losses in a row, a defense that can't stop the run, and a season that was supposed to be about contending suddenly needing a rescue.

Tonight's NBA card is stacked: Boston at Denver on a road win streak, Philadelphia at Orlando, Oklahoma City hosting Atlanta. Celtics-Nuggets is the pick.
