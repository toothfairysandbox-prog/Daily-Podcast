Russia hit two major bridges over the Dnipro River in Kyiv overnight, the mayor says, knocking out heating and water for parts of the capital as temperatures drop. It's part of a wider wave of drone and missile strikes that also hit Cherkasy and Kharkiv regions. Ukraine responded by hitting a key bridge into Crimea with drones, a retaliatory message, officials in Kyiv say, that if Russia hits Ukrainian bridges, Ukraine can hit Russia's. Separately, Ukraine accuses Russia of a "double-tap" strike — hitting a site, then hitting it again once rescuers arrived — that killed 72 people in southern Ukraine; recovery crews say continued shelling is hampering efforts to pull bodies from the rubble. All this comes as negotiators for Kyiv, Europe and the US were meeting on the shape of future ceasefire talks, talks that look harder to sustain with each new strike.

Donald Trump is in Asia for talks that could be historic for reasons beyond trade: a planned three-way meeting with China's Xi Jinping and Russia's Vladimir Putin on nuclear issues and the Ukraine war, with a possible Kim Jong Un sideline meeting still floated. He arrives in a region already on edge — in Manila, hundreds have been arrested in protests against Trump's visit for the Asean summit, with anger over the Iran war and a new US-Philippines AI pact; organisers say the next round could draw ten thousand people.

In France, unions and students are staging fresh nationwide strikes and protests Tuesday over wages, five months before a presidential election, part of a discontent commentators are calling a broader national malaise. And five EU countries — Germany, Austria, the Netherlands, Denmark and Greece — have announced plans for migrant "return hubs" in Rwanda, a policy already drawing legal challenges and accusations of moral failure.

The slower story: Israel's political landscape is shifting as a bloc led by former general Gadi Eisenkot moves to form a government courting ultra-Orthodox parties, even as the war in Gaza and settler violence in the West Bank continue — a woman was killed and several injured in a settler shooting near Jericho this week.
