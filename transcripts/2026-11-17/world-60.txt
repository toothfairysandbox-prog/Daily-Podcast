Russia struck two major bridges in Kyiv overnight, cutting heating and water for parts of the capital, as part of a wider missile and drone barrage. Ukraine hit back, striking a key bridge into Crimea with drones. Separately, Ukraine accuses Russia of a "double-tap" strike that killed 72 people in the south, hampering recovery efforts. It all comes as ceasefire negotiators for Kyiv, Europe and the US were meeting.

Meanwhile, Donald Trump is in Asia for a planned three-way meeting with China's Xi Jinping and Russia's Vladimir Putin on nuclear issues and the Ukraine war, arriving amid protests in Manila over his visit and a new AI pact.
