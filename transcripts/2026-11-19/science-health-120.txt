A cardiologist writing in STAT says he's puzzled by the new STREAM trial, which is getting blockbuster coverage for suggesting older patients can safely stop statins. His read: the findings don't actually support headlines telling people to quit their medication. This is not a reason to change anything you're taking — that's between you and a doctor, weighing your own risk.

New brain research finds that neural activity during free choices looks surprisingly similar to activity during forced decisions, building up before the action happens. It challenges simple notions of free will, though the researchers note personal preference still shapes outcomes. One study, contentious territory — worth watching for replication.

Researchers tested 212 plant-based foods sold in the UK — meat alternatives, oat and almond and soy milks — and every single one contained at least one mycotoxin, a fungal toxin common in grains generally. Alarming-sounding, but the study doesn't establish the levels found are actually harmful.

And in mice, excessive exercise without recovery left dopamine elevated in a brain region tied to memory and emotion for 48 hours afterward, a possible clue to overtraining symptoms like insomnia and anxiety in athletes — early, animal-only evidence.
