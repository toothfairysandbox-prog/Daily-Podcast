Luka Doncic finally hit a wall. After weeks of forty and fifty point games, he looked human against the Clippers — legs gone, shots short — and the Lakers lost. One game, but the first real crack in an otherwise absurd stretch.

Elsewhere, the Wizards snapped an eight-game skid against a depleted Raptors team, the Sixers erased a seventeen-point deficit to beat Orlando shorthanded, and the Rockets beat the Pacers a hundred and nineteen to a hundred and four but lost center Alperen Sengun to injury. The Nets are three and eleven with no real identity. Jayson Tatum is pushing back on claims he threw the ball at a referee during Boston's loss to Denver.

Worth tracking: Damian Lillard's return from his Achilles tear is already reshaping Portland, even short of his peak form.

In football, Dallas looks unsettled — Quinnen Williams dodged every question about a defensive play-calling change a dozen times in one press conference. Thursday night football is Indianapolis at Houston. Saturday brings England facing the All Blacks with nine changes, and Ireland's Dan Sheehan starting out of position at number eight against South Africa. Tonight, Edmonton visits Tampa Bay — a real Stanley Cup Finals preview, and the one worth watching.
